1. **Optimize HHI Calculation in `NB_Curadoria_Gold.Notebook`:**
    - The calculation of the HHI metric in `NB_Curadoria_Gold.Notebook/notebook-content.py` triggers multiple `.collect()` actions on the same Spark DataFrame (`df_carteira_ativa` and its derived forms). This forces Spark to execute multiple jobs and full passes over the data.
    - Specifically, `total_portfolio_row`, `hhi_cedente_row`, and `hhi_sacado_row` all trigger independent `.collect()` calls.
    - We will optimize this by using a single `.collect()` or, even better, computing these metrics within Spark without collecting intermediate values to the driver, or caching `df_carteira_ativa` before the actions and unpersisting it right after. The best approach to minimize collect calls is to use PySpark's CrossJoin to compute everything inside Spark or doing a `.cache()` on `df_carteira_ativa` since it's used multiple times (count, sum, grouped aggregations). Wait, crossJoin is bad. We can `cache()` `df_carteira_ativa`, compute the total, extract it, and then do the grouped sum. Actually, since `total_portfolio_row` is just a scalar, we can extract it as `first()[0]` or `collect()[0][0]` and use it in `lit(total_portfolio_value)`. To prevent three scans, we should add `.cache()` on `df_carteira_ativa` before calculating `total_portfolio_value`, `hhi_cedente_row`, and `hhi_sacado_row`, and `unpersist` it afterwards, in accordance with the memory: "To prevent redundant full table scans and repeated Catalyst plan evaluations in PySpark when executing multiple actions (e.g., count(), max(), and filtered sum()) on the same DataFrame, explicitly call .cache() before the actions and .unpersist() immediately after to clear memory."
